A changing world for students

 

The internet is an incredible resource that enables students to learn, connect and be creative in so many life-enhancing ways.

The digital landscape is changing rapidly, however. Students’ negative online behaviours, the volume of unsafe content and the rise in student-owned devices have accelerated dramatically since the pandemic meaning children need greater protection and support if they are to navigate their digital lives in a safe and self-directed manner.

Your school may have already experienced this shift and be considering how best to respond.

Free government provision is important but doesn’t give schools or their students the level of support needed to address today’s challenges. It’s time to raise the bar.
