Using AI in Schools, Colleges and MATs:
Best Practice Checklist

This checklist is designed to support school, college and MAT leaders in making informed decisions on artificial intelligence and its use in their settings.

1. A Whole-School/College/MAT Approach

1. Have you read the relevant materials from the DfE?
2. Do you have a consistent AI platform or platforms that are used across your education setting?
3. Have you surveyed your staff to understand how they currently use AI, both professionally and personally?
4. Have you set up an AI working party consisting of all stakeholders to support the strategic approach?
5. Do you have regular check-ins or meetings to discuss the approach as it develops or is implemented?
Please answer all questions before proceeding.

2. Policies & Risk Assessments

Policies

6. Do you have an AI Policy?
7. Has this been cross-referenced with other policies (Acceptable Use, Code of Conduct, Safeguarding, etc.)?

Risk Assessments

8. Have you assessed the risks of approved AI tools to understand how they may be used/misused?
9. Are further risk assessments carried out every time a new AI tool is introduced?
Please answer all questions before proceeding.

3. Technical Safeguards

Filtering

10. Have you applied your policy regarding the AI tools you are using to your filtering?
11. Does your web filtering currently block harmful AI content (including image and video) the moment it appears online?
12. Can you control or restrict access to AI chatbots by age group or role?
13. Do you have clear visibility and reporting on AI-related activity to support safeguarding decisions?

Monitoring

14. Does your monitoring solution pick up AI-related safeguarding concerns?
15. Can your monitoring identify subtle warning signs in the ways students interact with AI tools and chatbots?
16. Does your monitoring minimise false positives so staff are alerted only when meaningful intervention is needed?
17. Is your cloud storage regularly checked for inappropriate/harmful AI-generated content?
Please answer all questions before proceeding.

4. Education & Expectations

Staff Training & Expectations

18. Have all staff received training relating to the code of conduct and expectations of the use of AI?
19. Have staff received training on how to use AI tools effectively?
20. Are all staff aware of your data protection policy and how this applies to the use of technology, including AI tools?

AI Literacy for Staff and Students

21. Do you have a clear assessment and homework policy that is communicated to students and staff regarding the use of AI tools?
22. Have you considered how you will provide age-appropriate AI education for different age groups?
23. Do you know what tools you are going to use with the students to explore this topic?
24. Do you understand how these AI tools are going to be used, and what awareness staff and students should have when using them?
Please answer all questions before proceeding.

5. Community Engagement

25. Have you delivered information, advice and guidance for parents and carers about the use of AI?
26. Do you regularly share updates with parents regarding all aspects of digital safeguarding including AI?
Please answer all questions before submitting.
